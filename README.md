## Abstract

We introduce Maximum Hidden Irreversibility (MHI), a geometric invariant that isolates antisymmetric (rotational) components of hidden-state Jacobians in neural networks. MHI reveals latent rotational dynamics invisible to standard loss, divergence, and spectral diagnostics, and rises prior to emergent grokking.

## Why this matters

Systems with identical contraction and loss can exhibit fundamentally different global learning dynamics.  
MHI exposes hidden rotational structure that enables latent alignment before rapid generalization.
